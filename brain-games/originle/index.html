    How to play

    Guess the country today's guest comes from.

    Listen to their first clue. Choose Reveal next clue whenever you need more help. There are 8 clues, and they get easier. The last one shows the outline of their country.

    You have three guesses. Scores are out of 10,000: each clue you reveal costs more than the one before, from 250 points to 1,750 for the last, and each wrong guess costs 30% of your points. Miss all three, or give up, and you still score for how close your nearest guess came.

    Use the globe to keep notes: tap a country to shortlist it, tap again to cross it out.

    A new guest every day at midnight. Want more? Practice brings out a new guest whenever you like, and the Archive has every past daily.
