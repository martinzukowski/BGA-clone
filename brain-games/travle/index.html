      How to play

      Travel from the start country to the end country by naming every country you'd walk through on the way. You win once your countries join the two up, so try to do it in as few guesses as possible.

      ✅Perfect: gets you closer, and joins up with your route from the start. 🟩Good: gets you closer, but isn't joined to your route yet. 🟧Close: doesn't get you closer, but it's at most one country off the best route. 🟥Detour: takes you well off course.

      Countries that aren't joined to the start yet show grey on the map. You get the shortest route's length plus a few spare guesses. Up to three hints are free, but they show on your result.

      Borders: land borders count, and so do bridges and tunnels (Channel Tunnel, Øresund Bridge, the Bahrain and Singapore causeways). Exclaves don't: Kaliningrad doesn't join Russia to Poland, French Guiana doesn't join France to Brazil, and Gaza doesn't join Egypt to the rest of Palestine.
