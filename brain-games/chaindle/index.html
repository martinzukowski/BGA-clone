How to play

Find a word that links to the words you're given. Each word grows a chain toward yours: the stronger the connection, the longer the chain. Join them all the way for full marks.

  1. Rounds 1–3: two words. pizza + pasta → tomato
  2. Round 4: three words, one link for all of them. car + wheel + human → driver
  3. Round 5: one word, type its closest relative. video → footage

You get one guess per round. Each round is worth up to 1,000 points, 5,000 in all. A word we can't link to scores 0 for that round. Typos and plurals are forgiven.

The daily puzzle is the same for everyone and changes at midnight. Want more? Practice deals five fresh rounds every time you replay.
